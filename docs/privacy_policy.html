Fingerlette

Privacy Policy

✓ No data collected

Overview

Fingerlette is a local multiplayer party game. It runs entirely on your device and does not require an internet connection, an account, or any permissions.

Data We Collect

We collect absolutely no data. Fingerlette does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data of any kind.

Permissions

The app requests no device permissions. It does not access:

  • Camera or microphone
  • Location
  • Contacts or photos
  • Network / internet
  • Any other sensitive data

Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, crash reporting tools, or tracking libraries.

Children's Privacy

Fingerlette is suitable for all ages. Since no data is collected, the app complies fully with COPPA and similar children's privacy regulations.
