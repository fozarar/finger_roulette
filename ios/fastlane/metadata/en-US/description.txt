HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose how many players (2–5) and how many winners.
2. Everyone puts a finger on the screen and holds.
3. The circles light up one by one, then stop on the winner.

PERFECT FOR
• Deciding who pays the bill
• Picking who goes first in a board game
• Choosing a driver, a volunteer, or a loser
• Settling any argument between friends
• Party games and friendly dares
• Keeping kids happy without a coin toss
