Who pays the bill? Who goes first? Who does the dishes?

Stop arguing. Put your fingers on the screen, or type the names and spin the wheel.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose what you need: a winner, a loser, teams, or a turn order.
2. With fingers: everyone holds a finger on the screen, up to 5 people.
3. With names: type up to 20, then spin the wheel or split them into up to 4 teams.

PERFECT FOR
• Deciding who pays the bill
• Splitting a crowd into fair teams
• Picking who goes first in a board game
• Choosing a driver, a volunteer, or a loser
• Settling any argument between friends
• Party games and friendly dares
• Keeping kids happy without a coin toss

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
• Completely fair — everyone has exactly the same chance
• Keep several name lists, so every group is one tap away
• Share the result as an image, ready for your story
• Instant, no setup, no account, no internet needed
• Sound and vibration build the tension before the reveal
• Clean, dark design that looks good on any phone
