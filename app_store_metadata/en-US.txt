DESCRIPTION  (872/4000)
Who pays the bill? Who goes first? Who does the dishes?

Stop arguing. Put your fingers on the screen and let chance decide.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose how many players (2–5) and how many winners.
2. Everyone puts a finger on the screen and holds.
3. The circles light up one by one, then stop on the winner.

PERFECT FOR
• Deciding who pays the bill
• Picking who goes first in a board game
• Choosing a driver, a volunteer, or a loser
• Settling any argument between friends
• Drinking games and party dares
• Keeping kids happy without a coin toss

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
• Completely fair — every finger has exactly the same chance
• Instant, no setup, no account, no internet needed

• Clean, dark design that looks good on any phone
• Free, with no ads interrupting the game

Put a finger down. Let luck do the rest.
